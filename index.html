Let's be honest

Running a small business can feel like doing five jobs at once.

If you own a small business, you've probably had one of those days where you worked from morning until night and still had a long list of things waiting for you.

Answering messages. Writing social media posts. Preparing quotes. Following up with customers. Organizing your week.

None of those things are necessarily difficult. They just take time. And when you're running a small business, time is one of the things you can never get enough of.

That's where AI can actually be useful.

A different way to look at AI

AI isn't just for programmers or big companies.

For a long time, I thought artificial intelligence was something complicated — something you needed technical skills to understand.

But that's not really how today's AI tools work.

You can use them in everyday language to help with everyday business problems.

Think of AI as an assistant.

You still make the decisions. You still run your business. AI simply helps you get some of the repetitive work done faster.

Questions people ask

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need technical skills to use AI? +

No. Many modern AI tools are designed to be used through normal language. You don't need to be a programmer to start using them for everyday business tasks.

Can AI really help a small business? +

Yes. The biggest opportunities are often simple tasks that happen repeatedly: writing, organizing, brainstorming, summarizing, preparing drafts and creating customer communications.

Will AI replace me or my employees? +

AI is better viewed as a tool that can assist with repetitive work. Important business decisions, customer relationships and final review should remain under human control.

Why would I pay for a course when there is free content online? +

Free content can be extremely useful. A structured course can be valuable when you want a clear learning path, practical examples and a single place to follow instead of searching through unrelated tutorials.

Is AI worth learning in 2026? +

AI tools are becoming increasingly common in business and professional workflows. Learning how to use them effectively can be a useful skill, especially when you focus on practical problems rather than trying to learn every new tool.

Do I have to buy anything to start using AI? +

No. There are free AI tools and free versions of many popular services. You can start experimenting before deciding whether a paid tool or course makes sense for you.
